## Platform

web

## Users

- Primary: recruiters and hiring managers screening Computer Engineering students for software engineering internships. They scan in seconds for who he is, his level, his availability, and proof of shipped work.
- Secondary: potential freelance clients looking for someone to build web apps.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Brexcel Joe M. Orias at brexcel.vercel.app. Success is a recruiter or client understanding who he is and what he has shipped within the first screen, then opening a project, downloading the CV, or getting in touch.

## Positioning

A Computer Engineering student who ships real, team-used software and the pipelines that deploy it: Software Engineer Trainee at Egoist Labs building CI/CD workflows, plus full-stack projects with real users.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from the CV, LinkedIn-style links, GitHub, and direct messages, on desktop and on phones.
- The downloadable CV lives at `public/Brexcel-Orias-CV.pdf`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js app, deployed on Vercel. GSAP is used for motion. No Three.js and no MDX.
- Hero title text is exactly "software developer". Loading screen text is "brexcel". The phrase "Computer Engineering" appears in the hero world.
- Confirmed status line: "Software Engineer Trainee at Egoist Labs · Computer Engineering, URS Antipolo, Class of 2027 · Open to internships and freelance".
- The Robinsons Mall RFID Parking System is not shown anywhere yet.
- Do not show the Unity AR thesis project, TaskFlow as a separate project, AWS, or n8n.

## Brand Commitments

- Owner-confirmed direction: the site is his desk / developer's notebook, including the desk-lamp light and dark toggle. The corporate ID-badge / lanyard hero is retired.
- Project names are spelled Synctask and UrsacHub.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real project screenshots in `public/assets/` for Synctask, UrsacHub, UrsacHub Social, DSA Workbench, Photo Booth, and the Egoist Labs pipelines.
- One portrait: `public/assets/profile.jpg`.
- Confirmed numbers: Egoist Labs deploys went from 15–30 minutes manual to 3–5 minutes automated; 5 automated quality gates; 13 workflow runs since his changes merged; led a 5-person team on UrsacHub Social; DSA Workbench used in a class of about 40 students.
- Not yet on hand: photos of his desk, the IoT RC car, or his laptop. Never fabricate users, clients, testimonials, metrics, or photos.

## Product Principles

1. Show real work before describing it.
2. Every claim is something an interviewer can verify.
3. Answer who, level, and availability in the first screen.
4. One signature moment per page, rooted in his own desk, not a borrowed trend.
